About IPFQ

Maintain and Restore People’s Functional Ability

World physiotherapy(R) describes physiotherapy as “A healthcare profession that provides services to develop, maintain and restore people’s maximum movement and functional ability”. Physiotherapists are movement specialists who work towards health and wellbeing of people from all walks of life through promotion, prevention, treatment/intervention and rehabilitation.

Indian Physiotherapy Forum Qatar is a dynamic healthcare professional organization of physiotherapists from India, living and working in Qatar.
